Our Story

One Person. One Mission.

R
Rea Rusiana
Founder & Developer
DTI Registered Cebu, Philippines

I'm Rea. I started WebCraft PH because I kept seeing the same problem everywhere in Cebu.

A sari-sari store owner who makes the best homemade chili garlic in the barangay — but nobody outside her street knows she exists. A freelance hairstylist who's booked every weekend through word of mouth — but has zero online presence. A small resort in the province with beautiful rooms — but when tourists search "Cebu resort" on Google, they find the big chains instead.

These businesses don't need a P50,000 website from a Manila agency. They don't need WordPress with 47 plugins that loads in 8 seconds. They need something fast, clean, and affordable — a website that actually gets them found by customers.

So I started building. CebuLandMarket was my first — a property listing platform for Cebu. Then CebuRentMarket for rentals. Then BAHAW Studio for streetwear. Then CebuAI Services, MyTalent.MyDream, SkillBloom. Six platforms, all live, all serving real users.

Every single one was coded by hand. No templates, no shortcuts. Just clean code that loads fast on Philippine mobile data.

That's what I want to build for your business too.
